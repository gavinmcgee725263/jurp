Why smash?

The Smash Method

More surface on the griddle means more Maillard reaction — the browning that turns beef into flavor. It's science you can taste.

  1. 01 Ball it fresh

    Fresh chuck is ground every morning and hand-rolled into loose 75-gram balls. Never pressed, never frozen.

  2. 02 Smash it hard

    Onto a 230 °C griddle, pressed paper-thin for ten seconds. Maximum contact, maximum crust.

  3. 03 Serve it hot

    Cheese melts on the flat-top, buns toast in beef fat, and your burger hits the counter in ninety seconds.
