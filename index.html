The Estate

Two slabs of travertine hold eleven rooms of glass above the Serra valley — a house built to do one thing: keep the evening.

Completed in 2024 to a design by Studio Ferro Levi, the villa faces due west along the ridge. Every principal room opens to the same horizon; the pool runs to its edge and stops, and the hills do the rest. Below, the valley folds away toward the sea. There are no neighbours in view. There is very little to hear.

    Arrival

    The drive turns, the hills open, and the house appears against the last of the sun.

    A house in three materials

    Roman travertine

    Quarried at Tivoli, laid in book-matched slabs. It holds the day's heat past midnight — the house is warm to the hand long after the sun is gone.

    Smoked oak

    Fumed, brushed, and finished with oil. Floors, joinery, and the long library wall are cut from the same twelve trees.

    Patinated bronze

    Every window frame, handle, and stair rail. Left unlacquered, it darkens a little each year the house is lived in.
